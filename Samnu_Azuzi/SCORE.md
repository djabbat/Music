---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

This score integrates three musical systems:

1. **Ze-MIM State Machine** — 8 compositional states driving the dramaturgical arc
2. **Gurian Polyphony** — authentic 3-voice structure (Mtkmeli + Modzakhili + Bani) from Georgian folk tradition
3. **Krimanchuli Technique** — Gurian yodel, UNESCO-recognized, with chest↔falsetto leaps and ornamental figuration

All vocal soloists must undergo 8–10 hours of Georgian polyphonic training before rehearsals. Krimanchuli passages are marked with ᴋ in the score.

### I. GILGAMESH — «I Am King» (3:52)
*From Act I, Scene 3 — Gilgamesh's declaration in Uruk*

**Source melody:** Chakrulo (Gurian, UNESCO Intangible Heritage)
**Key:** D (Gurian mode: 1,2,b3,#4,5,b6,b7)
**Tempo:** 84 BPM
**Orchestration:** 15 tracks

| Track | Part | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 0 | Violins I | Melody doubling |
| 1 | Violins II | Counterpoint (fourth below) |
| 2 | Violas | Harmonic fill (fifth below) |
| 3 | Celli & Basses | Foundation (octave below) |
| 4 | Flutes & Oboes | Melody doubling (octave up) |
| 5 | French Horns | Sustained harmony |
| 6 | Trumpets & Trombones | Fanfares on phrase entries |
| 7 | Timpani | Gurian rhythmic pulse (4/4 and 8/4) |
| 8 | Harp | Celestial accents |
| 9 | **MTKMELI** | Gilgamesh — baritone, main melody |
| 10 | **MODZAKHILI** | Harmony voice — third below mtkmeli |
| 11 | **BANI** | Bass drone — moves every 12 notes |
| 12 | **KRIMANCHULI** | Falsetto yodel — octave above, phrase peaks |
| 13 | **Krimanchuli grace** | Ornamental figure — rapid alternations |

**Dramatic arc (Ze states):**
```
PEAK → COMPLEX → BUILD → PEAK → DRAMATIC → RESOLVE
```

Gilgamesh declares his power. Then uncertainty ("two-thirds divine, one-third mortal"). Friendship with Enkidu. The campaign to the Cedar Forest. Victory over Humbaba. Hubris — the seed of tragedy.

## KRIMANCHULI NOTATION

Krimanchuli passages are marked with these symbols in the vocal score:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ᴋ | Begin krimanchuli technique |
| ↑ | Falsetto leap (octave up) |
| ↓ | Return to chest voice |
| ◌ | Grace note ornament |
| ⟐ | Rapid chest↔falsetto alternation |

Example from Gilgamesh aria, bar 12:
```
ᴋ  ↑              ↓     ◌    ⟐
G4--C5--E5--C5--G4--A4--C5-D5-C5
I  am  king  of  U--ruk, the  strong...
```
